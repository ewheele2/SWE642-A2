Computer Science Graduate Department



About the Program

The Computer Science department at George Mason University offers several graduate learning options. We offer Masters Degrees, Doctoral, and Graduate Certificates. Our graduate programs combine rigorous academics with opportunities for innovation, research, and industry collaboration, equipping graduates to excel in both academic and professional careers.

The Computer Science department offers three Masters Degrees: Computer Science, Information Systems, Software Engineering. In addition to those, the Computer Science department offers a PhD in Computer Science. If you are looking for a graduate certificate because you are transitioning into a new field, or need extra knowledge to complement your own, we offer Computing Foundations and Software Engineering.

To get additional information on requirements, admissions, and if you need to request information, please visit our Graduate Programs page.
